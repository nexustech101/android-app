# SiteSnap privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

SiteSnap is a job-site photo app. This policy explains what it does with your data.

## What SiteSnap stores on your phone

- Photos you take in the app, saved to your phone's Pictures/SiteSnap folder, one folder per job.
- The job details you type in (job name, address, work order number) and the app's settings.

This stays on your phone unless you connect Google Drive.

## Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, SiteSnap uploads your job photos into a SiteSnap folder in your own Google Drive.

SiteSnap asks only for the `drive.file` permission. That means it can see and change only the files and folders it created. It cannot read anything else in your Drive.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time in SiteSnap's Settings, or remove access at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## What SiteSnap does not do

- No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Your photos and job details are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. They go only to your own Google Drive, if you connect it.
- Nothing is sold or shared.

## Updates

SiteSnap checks this project's GitHub releases page for new versions. That request sends no personal data.
